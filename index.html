♟ Chocachol

Place or move tokens, trap the opponent and capture them in bulk!

⚙ Players & Token Count

Quick (both players):

Each turn: place a token from your hand on any empty point, or move a board token one step along a line, or jump over an enemy token to capture it. If your placed/landed token leaves a run of enemy tokens in a straight line between it and another of your tokens, all of them are captured at once.
